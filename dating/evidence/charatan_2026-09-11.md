## 1. THE FINDING WORTH THE HOUR — the "DC" stamp is not a date, and three sources disagree

Collectors routinely read **DC** on a Charatan shank as a period marker. The summaries carry
three incompatible origins for it:

| Claim | Attributed to |
|---|---|
| DC (Double Comfort bit) introduced **in the 1950s**, per Charatan's own records — "some report seeing them in earlier production" | [Dating of Charatans — Pipedia](https://pipedia.org/wiki/Dating_of_Charatans) |
| DC **introduced by Lane in 1960** | same page, given as a competing account |
| "DC" stamping after the shape number **introduced when Dunhill took control, 1979** | [Charatan Pipes: History, Eras & Collector's Guide — alpascia.com](https://www.alpascia.com/moments/en/detail/81/charatan-pipes) |

**They disagree, and the spread is about twenty-five years.** I am not picking a winner.

The same material carries its own warning, which is the useful part:
> the DC bit "is not an accurate way to date a pipe because many Charatan's were made with
> regular and saddle type bits throughout the Lane Era."

**Our reading (a rule, not a date): DC describes a bit, not a period. It is a feature, not a
mark.** Under our own law, a feature is never spoken as a stamp. If this survives
verification it belongs in the Charatan cabinet as a *caveat*, not as a dating fact — and it
is the kind of correction that makes us useful, because the market uses DC as a date.

## 2. Stamp changes that, if verified, ARE testable on the pipe

| Feature you can see | Claimed period | Attributed to |
|---|---|---|
| "Made by Hand in City of London" in three lines | until **1979** | [Dating of Charatans — Pipedia](https://pipedia.org/wiki/Dating_of_Charatans) / alpascia summary |
| "CHARATAN'S MAKE / LONDON ENGLAND" in three lines | **after** that change | same |
| CP logo with the **C penetrating the P** | up to the **end of 1980** | same |
| CP logo with **C and P separate** | after the end of 1980 | same |
| Serif capital **L in a circle** (resembles £), the Lane importer mark | **1955 onward**, US-market models | [Charatan — Pipedia](https://pipedia.org/wiki/Charatan) |

⚠️ One more claim appears in the summaries and **we must not use it as written**: that the
£-like symbol "was absent from the shank from the end of 1980 approximately." Read forward
that is fine — *presence* suggests pre-1981. Read backward it dates a pipe by what is
missing, which our law forbids, and the same sources say the factory often simply forgot the
stamp: "both the English factory or Lane themselves sometimes, or perhaps even often forget
to stamp the L on a pipe," and a missing L "could also mean the pipe was destined for the
European market."

**Rule for our cabinet: the L present → Lane-era evidence. The L absent → tells us nothing.**

## 3. Company timeline — and a second disagreement, on the Dunhill year

| Event | Year | Attributed to |
|---|---|---|
| Lane takes sole US distributorship | 1955 | [Charatan — Pipedia](https://pipedia.org/wiki/Charatan) |
| Lane buys the company | "about 1955 or so" | same |
| Supreme S introduced | 1957 | same |
| **Lane's heirs sell Charatan to Dunhill** | **1978** | [History of Charatan — Smokingpipes.com](https://www.smokingpipes.com/smokingpipesblog/single.cfm/post/history-charatan) summary |
| **Dunhill takes control** | **1979** | [alpascia.com](https://www.alpascia.com/moments/en/detail/81/charatan-pipes) summary |
| Prescot Street factory closes | March 1982 | Pipedia summary |
| Dunhill sells the name, trademark and shape chart | 1988 | Pipedia summary |
| Charatans made by Butz Choquin, St Claude | 1988–2002 | Pipedia summary |
| Dunhill repurchases the name | 2002 | Pipedia summary |

**1978 versus 1979 for the Dunhill takeover is a real conflict** and it matters, because the
stamp changes above are pinned to it. Both go in the cabinet with both sources. Note also
that "Lane era" is given as "approx. 1955 to 1979–1984?" — with the question mark in the
source. Our bracket should be at least as wide as their uncertainty.

**Pre-1955 is structurally hard and we should say so publicly:** "Charatan records are almost
non-existent before Lane due to a factory fire." Absence of records is a fact about the
archive, not a date for a pipe.

## 4. James Upshall — the neighbouring find (same workshop lineage)

Charatan and Upshall are one story: **James Upshall was founded in 1978 by Barry Jones and
Ken Barnes, both formerly Charatan carvers** ([James Upshall — Pipedia](https://pipedia.org/wiki/James_Upshall)).
So the Dunhill takeover and the Upshall founding are the same moment in the same trade.

One physically testable claim, which is the valuable kind:
> "Tilshead Pipe Company acquired a New Hermes engraving machine about **August of 1983** and
> the James Upshall nomenclature, the grade (usually) and the stem logo were **engraved** from
> that time onward."
and, possibly describing the same change with a different date:
> after around **1984** the nomenclature was applied with a pantograph, "carving" into the
> stummel and revealing naked briar, where before it was **stamped** conventionally.

**Stamped versus engraved is something you can see with a loupe and no catalogue.** If it
verifies, it splits Upshall production at roughly 1983–84. The two dates given (Aug 1983,
~1984) may be one event or two; I am not resolving it.

Grades, for the cabinet: Tilshead (cosmetic seconds, no fills), then **S, P, B, G, E, X, XX**.
US grades, 1992 and earlier: **R, A, S, P, B, G, E, X, XX, XXX**.

### ⚠️ Two claims I am refusing on our own law
The summaries state, twice, rules that date a pipe by what is missing:
- "Pipes that do not feature a date stamp were not made until at least 1979."
- "The lack of a size stamp and an 'FH' stamp indicates something not added to James Upshall
  pipes until after 1989."

**Rejected. Absence never dates.** An unstamped pipe is a quiet pipe. These belong in the
cabinet only inverted: *presence* of a date stamp, a size stamp or FH is evidence; absence is
not. This is exactly the error our engine exists to correct, and it is sitting in the
reference the whole market reads.

### The local side already holds the harder fact
A local session banked founder testimony: **Ken Barnes, on tape, that the Upshall "78" year
stamp means exactly 1978** ("we did only 1978"). That is founder-primary and outranks
everything above. It is on the PC, not here.
