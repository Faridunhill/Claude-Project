# THE INSURANCE, AND THE LIST
### Two plans, with steps, and who does each one

**For:** Farid · **Date:** 2026-08-02
**Why this exists:** Farid asked for the details. These are the two cheapest, highest-value moves
in the whole plan, and neither is a technology problem.

---

## THE SITUATION, PLAINLY

Farid has **5,000 friends on a personal account, all pipe collectors**, plus a page and a group,
built over **twelve years**. It works. It is his strongest asset and probably his real edge.

**And every bit of it lives inside Meta's house.** Not a policy risk — an ownership one. An account
can close for any reason or none, and twelve years of relationships end that afternoon. There is
no appeal that reliably works and no export that brings the people back.

The two plans below do not reduce how much he uses social. They make it **survivable**.

---

# PART A — THE INSURANCE

**The rule, in one line: nothing is posted to Facebook that does not exist on faridunhill.com first.**

Not a second version. The same content, published on his own domain first, then shared to the
community with a link home.

### Why this works

- If Meta ever closes the account, **the work still exists.** Every pipe, every write-up, every
  video, on ground nobody can take.
- The link home turns a Facebook post into **a visit to his own site** — where the email form is.
  Part A is what feeds Part B.
- v1.0 already ordered it: *"owned platforms first, borrowed second. Anything on borrowed ground
  must exist on owned ground first."* This is not new policy; it is the existing law applied.

### Who does what

| Step | Who | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MACHINE** | Farid lists a pipe → the Monster writes the site entry, the copy, and (once built) the video |
| 2 | **MACHINE** | Publishes to faridunhill.com **first**, records `published · site` |
| 3 | **MACHINE** | Writes the post text for the community **with the link home already inside it** |
| 4 | **FARID** | Copies that post to his account / page / group. Ten seconds |
| 5 | **MACHINE** | Records `published · social`, and starts counting |

**Farid's total work: paste a post.** Everything else is already automatic — the Monster refuses
to publish to borrowed ground before owned ground; that rule is already in the code and tested.

### What still needs building

A small piece: the Monster writes site, Etsy and eBay copy, but not yet **the social post text
with the link**. That is a short job in the Maker, and the video sits beside it.

# PART B — THE LIST

**The goal: 5,000 collectors reachable without asking Meta's permission.**

v1.0 already ruled on this: *"eBay customer list: intelligence only, NOT a mailing list (struck
permanently). Own-domain email capture is the legal replacement."* This is that replacement,
built the clean way from the first day.

---

## STEP 0 — BEFORE ANYTHING: check you are allowed to mail

**Do this first. It costs one email and it can save months.**

Most email services publish restricted-industry lists, and tobacco is commonly on them. I tried to
read Mailchimp's prohibited-content page to confirm and **their site blocked me, so I do not know
their current position on pipes.** I am not going to guess about the thing everything else rests on.

**What Farid does:** write to the support or compliance team of the service he is considering, in
one short message:

> *"I sell collectable estate smoking pipes — vintage briar pipes for collectors, no tobacco and
> no nicotine products. Subscribers opt in on my own website with double confirmation. Is my
> business permitted under your acceptable use policy?"*

Get the answer **in writing** before collecting a single address.

**Ask two or three providers.** The one that says yes clearly, in writing, is the one to use. If
none will, the fallback is self-hosted sending, which is more work but nobody can switch it off.

**Why this is step zero:** a list you are not allowed to mail is worse than no list — it is months
of trust collected and then stranded.

---

## STEP 1 — decide what they get

Not "a newsletter". Nobody wants a newsletter. A collector wants **first refusal**.

**The recommended offer: FIRST LOOK.**

> *See every new pipe 24 hours before it goes on eBay or Etsy.*

It costs nothing, it is genuinely valuable to a collector, it is honest, and it directly serves the
business: the keenest buyers see stock first, and Farid learns what they want before the wider
market ever sees it.

Second option, weaker but useful: the dating knowledge — an honest bracket for their own pipe.

**Do not offer a discount.** It attracts bargain hunters, not collectors, and it trains the wrong
audience.

---

## STEP 2 — the page on faridunhill.com

One page. Short. It needs exactly these parts, and **every one of them is a legal requirement, not
a preference**, because his buyers are in the USA and Europe (CAN-SPAM and GDPR both apply):

1. **What they get, in one sentence.** *"New pipes, 24 hours before anyone else."*
2. **Email field.** Nothing else. No name, no phone, no address — the Well holds no personal data
   it does not need (M4), and every extra field loses subscribers.
3. **A consent checkbox, UNCHECKED by default.** Pre-ticked boxes are not consent under GDPR.
   > ☐ *Yes, email me when new pipes arrive. I can unsubscribe at any time.*
4. **A link to a short privacy note** — what is stored, why, and how to be removed.
5. **After they submit:** *"Check your email and click the link to confirm."*

---

## STEP 3 — double opt-in, and what gets recorded

**Double opt-in means:** they type their email → they receive an email → they click a link → only
then are they on the list.

**This is not optional for Farid.** It is what makes the list defensible if it is ever questioned,
it keeps out typos and fake addresses, and it is precisely the protection the struck eBay list
never had.

**Recorded with every subscriber** (doc 004, Amendment B):

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| `captured_at` | when they asked |
| `source_page` | where they came from — which is also how he learns which pipe brought them |
| `consent_text_version` | exactly what they agreed to, since the wording changes over time |
| `double_opt_in_confirmed_at` | **no row without this is mailable, ever** |

**The machine enforces it at send time, not at judgement time.** No unconfirmed address can be
mailed, even by accident.

## STEP 4 — how the 5,000 actually get there

**First, what NEVER happens.**

> **Do not export, scrape, copy or buy email addresses from Facebook, from the group, or from
> anywhere else. Not once. Not "just to start".**

It breaks Meta's terms, it breaks GDPR, it can end the ESP account, and **it is the exact mistake
that got the eBay list struck permanently.** A list must be built by people choosing to join it.
There is no shortcut, and the shortcut is what destroys the asset.

**Now, what does happen — five ways, all honest, in order of strength:**

1. **Every post links home.** Part A already does this. Someone interested clicks, lands on the
   pipe, sees the form. This is the engine; the rest is decoration.
2. **A pinned post** on the page and in the group: *"I now send new pipes to a small list a day
   early. If you want first look, it is here: [link]."* One post, pinned, working forever.
3. **When someone comments "is it still available?"** — the most common comment he gets — reply
   with the answer **and** the link. Those people are the best subscribers on earth: they have
   already raised a hand.
4. **In the sold-entry on his site.** Every sold pipe's page ends with *"want to see the next one
   first?"* — the entries stay live, so this works while he sleeps.
5. **In the package.** A small printed card with every pipe he ships. A buyer holding a pipe he
   loves is the warmest moment there is.

**Realistic expectation, stated honestly:** of 5,000 friends, a first-look list will collect
**perhaps 200–600 over the first year** — a few percent, gathered slowly. That is normal, and 300
collectors who chose to be there are worth more than 5,000 who did not.

---

## STEP 5 — what the Monster does with it

The machinery already exists.

- `email_capture` is already a Scale event; the code accepts it today.
- Each capture records the page it came from — so the dig can eventually say *which pipes bring
  subscribers*, which no other data he has can answer.
- **The guard metric** (doc 004, Amendment B): the primary number is captures, and the guard is
  the **30-day return rate**. A lesson only confirms if captures rise **and** the guard does not
  fall. Without that pair, the machine would learn to optimise for pop-ups and produce a large
  dead list.

---

## STEP 6 — the first email, and the rhythm

**The first email decides everything.** It should be short, from Farid, not from a company:

> *You asked to see new pipes first. That is all this is — a few lines when something good
> arrives, a day before it goes public. If it ever becomes noise, the unsubscribe link is at the
> bottom and I will not be offended.*

Then: **one email per new batch of stock. Never more than weekly.** Every email carries the
unsubscribe link — legally required, and honest besides.

**What must never be sent:** anything with a dating claim that is not the engine's honest bracket.
The list is the audience that will check.

## THE ORDER TO DO THEM IN

1. **Step 0** — the written yes from an email provider. Nothing else starts until this lands.
2. **Part A** — post text with the link home. Small build, immediate value, and it feeds everything.
3. **Step 2** — the signup page.
4. Then the list grows on its own, from posts he was making anyway.

**Nothing here needs new spending.** It needs the written permission, one page, and one habit:
**site first, then the community, always with a link home.**
