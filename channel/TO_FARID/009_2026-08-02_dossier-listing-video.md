## 2. WHERE THE TOOL FITS IN THE MONSTER — exactly

v1.0 already says where video belongs:

> **THE MAKER (production)** — pages, listings, posts, **videos**. Only what the Judge ordered.

So this is not a new organ. It is a fourth output of the Maker, beside `site.md`, `etsy.txt` and
`ebay.csv`. The loop is unchanged:

```
Well ─► Digger ─► Judge ─► MAKER ─► Mouth ─► Scale ─► Well
                            │
                            └── site.md · etsy.txt · ebay.csv · video.mp4  ← new
```

**What the Monster supplies, and MoneyPrinterTurbo does not have to invent:**

| The video needs | The Monster already holds it |
|---|---|
| The script | brand, shape, material, price band from the Well, dating bracket (or UNDATED) |
| The pictures | Farid's own listing photographs |
| The voice | an ElevenLabs profile — MPT supports ElevenLabs |
| The order to make it | the Judge's standing decision, same as every twin |
| The stamp | the playbook version, so a retired lesson can be traced (N1) |
| The measurement | a `published` row per surface, and the clock that is already running |

**The one setting that matters:** MPT's default flow lets an LLM write the copy from a keyword.
For a dating encyclopedia that is the single most dangerous habit — a free-writing model will
state a year for a pipe it has never seen. **We use it as a renderer, never an author.** It takes
a finished script; it supports that; and it has a real API (FastAPI) and batch mode, so the
Monster can drive it per listing without a human clicking anything.

Technically it is a good fit: MIT licence, Python, runs on CPU, no GPU, own local media supported.

## 3. THE PROBLEM THAT IS BIGGER THAN THE TOOL

**TikTok explicitly restricts smoking pipes.** Its policies name *"smoking paraphernalia, such as
smoking pipes, rolling papers, filters… or any other device, equipment or apparatus designed for
smoking"* among restricted products, alongside tobacco itself. Its Branded Content Policy — in
force 31 August 2026 — prohibits tobacco and related products in commercial content.

Instagram carries similar tobacco restrictions. Independent studies find enforcement patchy, with
a great deal of tobacco content surviving — **but that is not a defence.** Patchy enforcement
means the risk is random, not absent, and a seller account posting product videos is exactly the
commercial content the rules describe.

**This is the same structural fact v1.0 already recorded**, now extending from paid to organic:

> *"ALL paid channels closed for Faridunhill (tobacco category). Organic is the only
> non-revocable road. Permanent, structural."*

Organic social for a pipe *shop* is less non-revocable than it looked. An account built for years
and removed in a day is exactly the borrowed-ground risk the Mouth law exists to prevent.

## 4. SO WHERE DO THE VIDEOS GO?

Ranked by risk, safest first:

| Destination | Risk | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **faridunhill.com** | none — owned ground | The Mouth law puts it first anyway. A video on the hub page cannot be taken down by anyone |
| **eBay listing video** | none — allowed on the platform where the sale happens | The most direct commercial value of the four |
| **Etsy listing video** | none — same reason | Same |
| **YouTube** | moderate — age restriction likely for tobacco | Collector/educational framing survives better than product-push |
| **TikTok / Instagram** | **high — explicitly restricted** | The audience is real, and so is the ban. **Farid's decision alone** |

**The recommendation: build the renderer, point it at owned ground and the marketplaces first.**
Those three carry no account risk and cover where the money actually is. Social is a separate
decision with a real cost attached, and it is his.

## 5. WHAT I DO NOT KNOW

- Whether MPT's ElevenLabs integration accepts a **specific saved voice profile**. Untested and
  undocumented, and it decides whether the voice can be ours.
- How its output looks built from **still photographs** rather than clips. Pipe photography is

- Whether enforcement on Instagram would actually reach an account this small. Unknowable, and
  guessing is how accounts are lost.
