## LIVE — my proposal, in the order I would run them (7)

**1. Fingerprint and scale every pipe, starting with the next one you photograph.**
*(H2.1 + H2.5 — the Indentation Layer and Scale Discipline.)*
Three to five macro shots of fixed zones — stamp, grain face, rim, stem logo,
button — plus **any object of known size in one frame** (a coin, a bank card).
This is the only idea in the entire drawer that is **destroyed by delay.** Every
pipe that leaves this house without those photos can never be captured again.
Everything else on this list costs the same next year. This one gets more
expensive every single day, and it costs you nothing but a photo routine.
What it unlocks later: "is this the SAME pipe?" — provenance you can prove,
theft recovery, anti-switcheroo on returns, and measurements in millimetres
instead of "appears large."

**2. Rename the Passport to THE DATING DESK and point it at condition + originality.**
*(H1.5 + H5.6.)* Your own data says nobody doubts the pipes are real — they want
to know *when* and *what is original*. "Dating Desk" says exactly what we do,
carries the Professor's face, and walks away from the certificate problem in a
way "Passport" never fully does. The engine, the PDF, and the 6-photo protocol
all survive unchanged. Only the promise on the door changes.

**3. Stamp-die forensics.** *(H2.2.)* This is the best idea I found in the sweep
and it is not in the drawer. Numismatics has dated coins by studying die wear for
a century. **Nobody has ever done it for pipe stamps.** Cluster our stamp macros
into die variants, bind each variant to a dated anchor — and our dating gets
sharper than any public table, our fake-detection gets photographic evidence, and
at the end we own "the die catalog," a citable reference asset. It is also the
exact thing that satisfies the level-3 professional who arrives with doubt.

**4. Publish "Our References" this week.** *(H1.3.)* The endless archive list. The
manifest is already committed to this repo — 19,180 rows. We publish *what we
hold*, never a single scanned page, so LAW 2 is untouched. It is the cheapest
credibility we will ever buy, and it makes the level-2 collector believe us before
he reads a word of an entry.

**5. Give the Marketing Monster its path.** *(H4.2.)* 900 lines, 26 passing tests,
eight of your laws written as code that fails if anyone drops them. It is blocked
on **one fact** — where the hub/cabinet data lives on your PC. Not a decision, a
path. A local session runs `inspect` (reads the header row, writes nothing), then
`load`, and the pipes dig starts. Your cost afterwards: about 15 minutes a week
answering a generated PENDING file with single words.

**6. The Book of Mistakes — the historical wing only.** *(H1.2.)* The assistant's
mistakes and viewers' confessions need the show to exist first. But **"what the
pipe world got wrong for a century, with sources"** needs nothing but the cabinets
and the honesty law we already run. It is encyclopedia content no competitor can
write, and it is the most shareable thing we could publish.

**7. Populate `brand` and `origin`.** *(H7.2.)* 2 of 264 product files have a
brand field. Every identification we have made lives trapped inside a product
*title string*. Until it moves into a field, no filter, no collection page, no
cabinet join, and no marketing engine can ever see it. Unglamorous; blocks five
other things.
