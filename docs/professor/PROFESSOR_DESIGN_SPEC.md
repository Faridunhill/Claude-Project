# The Professor — written design spec (locked BEFORE any pixel; v1, 2026-09-11)

This is the "words that cannot drift". Every master-portrait take is generated from the
reference kit + this text. Change the text first, then regenerate — never the other way.

## Identity (from the reference photos — these are anchors, not options)
- Man in his sixties, medium-warm skin, calm face, faint closed-mouth smile at rest.
- **Gold rectangular half-rim glasses** (thin gold frame on top, rimless below).
- **Short grey-white stubble beard**, fuller at the chin, moustache slightly darker.
- **Two small moles on the left cheek** (viewer's right) — keep them; they are identity.
- Short, close-cropped grey hair; broad forehead; strong straight nose; deep smile lines.
- Ears slightly prominent; neck lean.

## Character design (Farid's bible)
- **Elegant, slim.** Upright posture, unhurried hands.
- **Small Irish flat cap**, dark tweed (charcoal-brown herringbone), worn low but eyes visible.
- Dark waistcoat over a dark shirt; sleeves rolled at the bench; no tie.
- **A pipe in hand, never lit** — held by the bowl, or resting on the bench; never in the
  mouth on the master portrait (keeps the mouth free for lip-sync).
- Setting for the master: neutral warm workshop background, soft key light from the left,
  head-and-shoulders, eyes to camera.

## House style
Painterly 2D, soft shading, visible brush texture in shadows, no heavy black outlines,
warm palette (tobacco browns, brass, deep green), skin natural (not orange), 3:4 portrait,
2K or higher.

## Master-portrait prompt (Nano Banana Pro; attach REF_01–REF_05)
```
Using the five attached photos of the same man as identity references, paint him as an
animated character in painterly 2D style with soft shading and no heavy black outlines.
KEEP HIS LIKENESS: same face structure, same eyes and brow, same nose, same gold half-rim
rectangular glasses, same short grey stubble beard, the two small moles on his left cheek.
He is a distinguished professor of pipe craft and restoration, in his sixties, elegant and
slim. He wears a small dark tweed Irish flat cap, a dark waistcoat over a dark shirt with
sleeves rolled. He holds an unlit briar pipe by the bowl in his right hand, below his chin,
not in his mouth. Head and shoulders, eyes to camera, faint warm smile. Neutral warm
workshop background, soft light from the left. Portrait 3:4, high detail.
```
