# H1. THE PROFESSOR — ABSENT FROM THE DRAWER ENTIRELY
*Source: `docs/PROFESSOR_PROJECT.md` + `docs/PROFESSOR_FACE_PIPELINE.md` (main).*

### H1.1 The Assistant, and her arc
- **RECORDED:** A second character. Starts as an employee → falls in love with the
  pipe world through the professor → becomes highly skilled → **eventually earns
  her own episodes.** Her mistakes are the beginner mistakes; she is "the
  audience's body inside the show."
- **MY READ:** This is a retention engine disguised as a character. A viewer who
  watched her be bad at something has a reason to come back and watch her be good
  at it. No competitor in this space has a multi-season arc.

### H1.2 The Book of Mistakes
- **RECORDED:** Her errors collected into a book *or a dedicated wing of the
  encyclopedia*, each cross-referenced to the entry that corrects it. Grows in
  three directions: her mistakes · viewers' confessed mistakes ("send us the pipe
  you ruined") · **historical mistakes — what makers and smokers got wrong for a
  century.** Could become a physical/e-book "authored" by her character.
- **MY READ:** The third direction is the real asset. "What the pipe world got
  wrong for a hundred years, with sources" is an encyclopedia section no one else
  can write, and it is pure honesty-law content. The other two directions are free
  user-generated material with a consent line attached.

### H1.3 The References Corner — "Our References"
- **RECORDED:** A page listing *everything we hold*: sites mirrored, books,
  catalogues, old advertisements. "A long, beautiful list that looks like it will
  never end." The endless list **is** the brand promise.
- **MY READ:** Cheapest credibility purchase available. The `ark_manifest.csv` in
  this repo already holds 19,180 rows — the list can be generated today from data
  that is already committed. It publishes *titles we hold*, never scans, so LAW 2
  is untouched.

### H1.4 The three levels of collectors *(the theory under the whole business)*
- **RECORDED:** L1 beginner (wants easy answers) · L2 medium, ~60% of collectors
  (wants **dating** — "whoever holds the rare catalogue wins this level") ·
  L3 professional (comes with **doubt**, comes to test us; if satisfied he writes
  about us). **Level 3, the smallest group, is the marketing multiplier.**
- **MY READ:** This is the most strategically load-bearing paragraph in the whole
  repo and it is filed as a brainstorm note. It explains why the archive beats
  the storefront, why honesty beats confidence, and who the encyclopedia is
  actually written for. It should be a law, not a note.

### H1.5 Episode formats never run
- **RECORDED:** "New in the Archive" (the professor first-opening a rare
  catalogue = an episode + a list entry + a news hook) · **The Dating Desk** (a
  formal service page — "bring your pipe, the professor dates it") · The Clinic
  (viewers' damaged pipes examined on air) · Office Hours (viewer letters) ·
  nightly 30–60s mistake short · 8–15 min full restoration long-form.
- **MY READ:** The Dating Desk is not a video format — it is the Passport with a
  better name and a human face. "Dating Desk" also sidesteps the certificate
  problem in a way "Passport" does not.

### H1.6 The production wall, stated honestly
- **RECORDED:** "Talking-avatar tools can't manipulate objects — full-screen hand
  work needs real macro footage, animation, or hybrid. (Open question.)"
- **MY READ:** The show's premise is *hands on pipes*, and the tooling can only do
  a talking head. Every format above that needs hands is blocked on this one
  unanswered question. Farid's own hands on real macro footage + the professor's
  voice over them is the obvious hybrid, and it costs a phone and a tripod.

### H1.7 Voice + face upgrade paths
- **RECORDED:** ElevenLabs **Professional Voice Clone** (30 min–3 h of clean
  speech) as an upgrade over the approved instant clone. Character LoRA on ~30
  images *generated from the approved bible*, never on Farid's photos directly —
  the trap the first two LoRA attempts hit. Character sheet (front/¾/profile/
  expressions/full body) from `BIBLE.png`.
- **MY READ:** The PVC recording session is 3 hours of Farid reading pipe
  restoration passages. That is also raw material for narration, shorts, and the
  audio version of the encyclopedia. One session, three products.

# H2. THE EIGHT INVENTIONS — from the Object Identification council review
*Source: branch `claude/object-indentation-design-4pt3n7`,
`COUNCIL_REVIEW_IDENTIFICATION_DOCTRINE.md`. The drawer captured one of these
(F7 Grain Print) as a single line.*

| # | Invention | One line |
|---|---|---|
| H2.1 | **The Indentation Layer** | Macro photos of 3–5 fixed zones = an instance fingerprint. Answers *"is this the SAME object?"*, not *"what is this?"* |
| H2.2 | **Stamp-die forensics** | Treat maker's stamps like coin dies: cluster stamp crops into die variants, bind variants to dated anchors. Narrows dating past any public table |
| H2.3 | **The Negative Archive** | Verified fakes and *corrected wrong guesses* stored as first-class anchors, with what gave them away |
| H2.4 | **Layered comparison DB** | One vector index per layer (character / specification / mark / indentation) + an append-only evidence ledger — never one fused embedding |
| H2.5 | **Scale discipline** | Every intake photo includes a reference of known size. "Bowl height 47.2 mm ± 0.8" instead of "appears large" |
| H2.6 | **The interrogation loop** | Instead of surrendering to the human at low confidence, ask for the ONE photo that would resolve it |
| H2.7 | **The Identification Dossier** | The verdict, hashed and tamper-evident, attached to the listing and travelling with the object on resale |
| H2.8 | **Cross-examination passes** | Ask each narrow question by several independent routes; disagreement is recorded as evidence, not averaged away |

**Council's own build order:** DB+ledger → blind-first Stage 0 → scale discipline
→ **start capturing indentation photos NOW** → negative archive → the rest.

**MY READ, and it is the sharpest thing I found:**
- H2.1 and H2.5 are the only two ideas in this entire drawer that are **destroyed
  by delay**. Every pipe that leaves the house un-fingerprinted and un-scaled can
  never be captured again. Everything else can be built next year at the same cost.
- H2.2 (die forensics) is the one idea I can find no precedent for in this hobby.
  Numismatics has done it for a century; nobody has done it for pipe stamps. It is
  publishable, citable, and it is *exactly* the "did they really get it" proof that
  wins the level-3 collector in H1.4.
- H2.3 (negative archive) is the honesty law turned into an asset. We are already
  required to record corrections; this says stop deleting them and they become
  training data and a veto class.

# H3. THE ASHCOMBE AUTONOMY EXPERIMENT
*Source: branch `claude/red-team-strategic-eval-c4rn3s` —
`ASHCOMBE_COMMANDER_BRIEF_v1_0.md` + a 5-seat red-team/council document.*

- **H3.1 The Proof Log.** A sealed, hash-chained, timestamped decision log +
  a filed tax return = a verifiable artifact that an AI ran a business for a year.
  Brief calls it "the invention." **The red team's verdict was NO** on the
  invention claim, with one narrow conditional residual (the specific composite of
  filed return + sealed log). Locked council decisions: **Academy first**, brain on
  frontier cloud API, bookkeeping automated too, vision **bought not built**.
- **H3.2 The Academy** — the school + report card: tests the agent, catches
  mistakes, feeds corrections back. Called "the core of the build." Note: the
  Peterson branch's `HANDOVER.md` calls the Academy "the self-correction loop —
  the system's core principle" in a completely different system. **The same idea
  was invented twice, independently, in two branches.**
- **H3.3 The intervention ledger + guardrail file** — every human touch recorded;
  spend cap, price floor/ceiling, daily loss limit as a file, not a habit.
- **H3.4 The EYE data flywheel** — a page where a collector uploads 6 standard
  photos + info and gets a **free dating report**; their filled-in info IS the
  label. "Models can be copied, the dataset cannot." With a **consent checkbox
  from photo #1** — "without it the dataset is legally unusable."
- **H3.5 The storage plan** — 30 MB/collector; 1k reports ≈ 30 GB, 100k ≈ 3 TB.
  Phase 0 current drive → Phase 1 two mirrored 4 TB (~$200) → Phase 2 NAS
  (~$700–1000). **JPEG XL lossless for originals** (~20% smaller, reversible),
  AVIF/JXL lossy for processed copies, never lossy originals — "dating lives in
  tiny details and lossy compression smudges those first." Zip on photos: useless.
- **H3.6 Pre-registered protocol** — one dated, hashed, signed document stating
  what the experiment claims and how it is measured, *before* day 1.
- **MY READ:** H3.4 and B1/Pipe Passport are **the same page** described by two
  sessions that never met. Whichever name wins, it should be built once. And the
  red team already killed the headline claim — so what survives here is the
  flywheel and the guardrails, not the "nobody has this yet" framing.

# H10. BORN IN THIS BRAINSTORM (2026-07-31) — not from any branch

These did not exist in any file before this session. Recorded so they enter the drawer
properly instead of living in a chat log.

- **H10.1 The evidence cut** — the episode format where drawn ink panels carry the
  *story* and our own captioned photographs carry every *fact*. The honesty law made
  visual. It is also the answer to "how do you montage it."
- **H10.2 EVIDENCE vs KNOWLEDGE** — the line that decides what may be drawn. Evidence
  (stamps, hallmarks, date codes, nomenclature, a named pipe's grain) is never drawn.
  Knowledge (anatomy, airflow, generic damage, tools, repair steps) is free to draw.
  Applies far beyond video — it is a publishing rule for the whole encyclopedia.
- **H10.3 The prop bible** — ~70–90 assets commissioned once so per-episode art cost
  falls toward zero. The difference between a show and an engine.
- **H10.4 The defect library + capture-before-repair rule** — photograph every damage
  before fixing it, tagged by type. Farid's restorations already generate this and
  currently throw it away. Covers ~80% of cases by his estimate.
- **H10.5 Tag the existing 2,000+ photos by defect type** — the library may already
  exist inside ten years of listing photography. Zero new shooting.
- **H10.6 Crowdsourcing the rare 20%** — the defects Farid never sees arrive through
  the Book of Mistakes ("send us the pipe you ruined") and Dating Desk submissions,
  with consent. Two existing ideas that turn out to feed each other.
- **H10.7 X-ray / CT a real intact pipe** — the airway, draft-hole alignment and
  drilling quality of an *un-cut* pipe. Cheap at a vet or NDT shop, real evidence, and
  nobody in the hobby publishes it. An episode in itself.
- **H10.8 The layered vector cutaway master** — one anatomy asset with toggleable
  layers (airflow, filter variants, cake stages, burnout, mortise/tenon fit good vs
  bad, bad drilling, stem and bit types). Serves repair episodes weekly, not once.
- **H10.9 The multilingual term map** — pipe anatomy has one vocabulary per country
  (EN *bowl/shank/mortise/bit* · FR *foyer/tige/mortaise/tuyau/lentille*). No reference
  consolidates them. Would open organic reach in languages no English-only competitor
  occupies. Must be built from the cabinets and cited, never assumed from a diagram.
- **H10.10 Ask Bill Burney / A.S.P. for permission** — Pipedia carries his Pipe Parts
  Charts "by permission." So ask. Costs one letter; either we gain a credited
  professional reference or we lose a day. Opens a relationship with a respected figure
  either way. *"Buy, don't pirate" has a positive form: ask.*
- **H10.11 Photograph anything that arrives broken** — burnouts, split shanks, cracked
  stems are free cross-sections that require no tool and arrive on their own.
- **H10.12 The X-ray evidence track** *(Farid, 2026-07-31 — "I will do it for all premium
  pipes to prove honest and go up in the collector level ladder")* — per-pipe internal
  imaging as a published condition-and-originality badge. Standard museum conservation
  practice on wooden objects; proves hidden repairs, replaced tenons, drilling quality
  and wall thickness; **never dates and never authenticates.** Owning a machine is
  NJDEP-registrable with a mandatory safety survey, so the route is rent-before-own.
  Full research + sources: `docs/XRAY_EVIDENCE_TRACK.md`.
- **H10.13 The interior fingerprint** — an X-ray cannot be scraped from a listing; it
  can only be made by holding the object. The Indentation Layer (H2.1) applied to the
  inside of the pipe, where no competitor can follow.
