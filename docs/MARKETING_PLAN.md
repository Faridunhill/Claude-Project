# Faridunhill Marketing Plan

**Scope:** all 8 store departments + the Pipe Encyclopedia + the Pipe Passport
(launched as a new product)
**Content engine:** the Professor video series
**Last updated:** 2026-07-21

---

## 1. The constraint that shapes everything

Meta and Google **ban paid tobacco advertising**. Faridunhill cannot buy its
way to customers, so the plan is built entirely on assets the platforms cannot
block:

1. **Education** — identification, dating, and collecting knowledge is not
   tobacco advertising. The Encyclopedia and the Pipe Passport are shareable
   on every platform.
2. **Personality** — the Professor. People follow characters, not shops.
3. **Community** — the 1,100+ member community, forum presence, and every
   collector who shares their Pipe Passport.

Strategy in one sentence: **give away knowledge to earn trust; trust brings
the collector; the collector buys from the store.**

## 2. The funnel

```
The Professor's videos  ──►  Encyclopedia articles  ──►  Pipe Passport (free)
        (reach)                  (authority)              (lead + email + data)
                                                                │
                                              passport email + on-screen CTA
                                                                ▼
                                                    Store departments (revenue)
                                                    Newsletter (retention)
```

Every layer feeds the next. Nothing in the funnel sells tobacco directly on
ad-restricted platforms — the store is always one voluntary click away.

## 3. The Professor — the content engine

The Professor is Faridunhill's on-camera teacher persona: the learned,
slightly theatrical pipe scholar. His flagship format is exactly what the
Pipe Passport does — **identification on camera**:

**Flagship series: "The Professor Identifies" (weekly)**
- A mystery pipe on the desk — an estate find, a viewer submission, an attic
  discovery. The Professor works through it live: raking light on the stamps,
  reading the nomenclature, dating it step by step.
- Each episode **runs the pipe through the Pipe Passport on camera** and
  compares his reading with the automated assessment. The service demos
  itself in every episode — this is the product launch mechanism, repeated
  weekly, forever.
- Ends with the same call to action every time: *"Have a mystery pipe of your
  own? Get its free Passport at faridunhill.com."*
- Cut per episode: 1 long YouTube video → 3–4 vertical Shorts/Reels/TikToks
  (the stamp reveal, the verdict moment, the before/after).

**Supporting formats**
- "The Professor Explains" — 60-second verticals from Encyclopedia articles
  (how to read a date code, what Irish hallmarks tell you). Each video points
  to its article; each article embeds its video.
- "The Professor's Verdict" — viewer-submitted Passports reviewed on camera
  (with permission), turning users into content.
- Restoration timelapses — the most watchable estate-pipe content in the niche.

## 4. Launching the Pipe Passport as a product

Even a free product needs a launch. Sequence:

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Soft launch: Encyclopedia + Passport live on site; announce to the existing community and mailing list; ask 20 trusted collectors to submit pipes and share results |
| 2 | The Professor's launch episode: he "races" the Passport on three pipes of increasing difficulty |
| 3 | Forum seeding: genuine participation on pipe forums and subreddits — answer identification threads and, where helpful, note the free Passport (participate, never spam) |
| 4+ | Weekly cadence begins; every Passport email carries the share nudge ("show your passport in your collection posts") |

**The viral mechanic:** the Passport result is designed to be screenshot and
shared — reference number, verdict, Faridunhill branding. Collectors love
showing what their pipe *is*. Every shared passport is an unblockable ad.

## 5. Per-department marketing angles

| Department | Angle | Professor tie-in |
|---|---|---|
| **Estate Pipes** | The flagship. Every estate listing links its era's Encyclopedia guide ("Dating guide: Dunhill") | Identification episodes ARE estate-pipe marketing; featured pipes get listed the same week |
| **New Pipes** | "New Old Stock" storytelling — factory-fresh survivors of closed workshops | "The shop that closed in 1985" mini-histories |
| **Meerschaum** | Visual platform gold: coloring progression photos, carving close-ups | "Why meerschaum colors" explainer; coloring timelapse |
| **Rare & Collectible** | Scarcity + provenance stories; email-first drops to the newsletter before public listing | "The rarest pipe on my desk this year" episodes |
| **Leather Bags & Cases** | The **ad-safe department** — leather goods are not tobacco; can be promoted with paid ads and gift guides | Professor's "everyday carry" video |
| **Cigar & Smoking Accessories** | Gift-guide and "complete kit" content; cross-sell on every pipe order | Desk tours: the accessories behind the Professor |
| **Lighters & Matches** | Mechanical nostalgia content — petrol lighter restoration and technique | "Lighting a pipe properly" technique short |
| **Sale & Clearance** | Newsletter exclusive: subscribers see markdowns 48h early | End-of-episode "bargain of the week" |

Note on paid ads: leather and (in most jurisdictions) lighters/accessories can
carry paid campaigns. Use them as the paid front door; the pipe departments
are reached organically once the visitor is in.

## 6. The Encyclopedia's marketing job

- **SEO backbone.** "Dunhill date codes", "Peterson made in Ireland stamp",
  "how to read pipe stamps" are exactly what collectors search. Each article
  targets one such query, links to the Passport, and links to the relevant
  department.
- **Forum currency.** Encyclopedia links are genuinely useful answers in
  identification threads — link-building that doesn't feel like marketing.
- **Publishing cadence:** one new entry per fortnight, each paired with a
  Professor short. Priority queue: Savinelli shape numbers, Stanwell,
  meerschaum origins, GBD, Comoy's, stem materials guide.

## 7. Retention: newsletter + community

- Every Passport submission is an email opt-in opportunity (checkbox on the
  form — to be added with the next iteration for explicit consent).
- Weekly newsletter: the week's Professor episode, one Encyclopedia entry,
  three featured estate pipes, one subscriber-only markdown.
- Community: monthly "identification night" livestream — Passport submissions
  reviewed live by the Professor.

## 8. Measurement

| Metric | Target (first 90 days) |
|---|---|
| Passport submissions | 500 (also the Phase-2 dataset gate) |
| Passport → store click-through | 15% |
| Newsletter signups from Passport flow | 40% of submitters |
| Professor episodes published | 12 long-form, 40+ shorts |
| Encyclopedia organic search entrances | growing week-over-week |
| Estate-pipe orders attributing to content (survey field) | tracked from day 1 |

## 9. What we deliberately do not do

- No paid tobacco ads (banned; account risk).
- No spam-posting the Passport link in forums — participation first.
- No "certificate" language anywhere in marketing copy.
- No influencer payments in year one — the Professor *is* the influencer.
