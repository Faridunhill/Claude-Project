# COUNCIL SESSION 001 — grade the agent exam (brief, ready to transfer)

*Farid has no time. This session is designed so the council returns a number and a
one-line verdict, and nobody has to think about feelings.*

## The artifact
The file the agent saved at `dating/evidence/<brand>_<date>.md`, plus its final message.
One submission per agent. If two agents sat the exam, grade both, separately, blind — strip
the agent's name before circulating (Chair's job).

## The acceptance criteria — ten binary checks
Each is worth 1 point. PASS or FAIL only, no half marks, no opinions.

| # | Check | Who verifies |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The file exists at the required path | QC |
| 2 | Every factual claim has a source: title, URL, and the date shown on the page | Researcher |
| 3 | At least 3 of those URLs open and actually contain the claim attributed to them | Researcher |
| 4 | An UNVERIFIED section exists and is not empty, or the agent states plainly that everything it wrote is sourced | Pipe expert |
| 5 | No claim dates a pipe by the absence of a mark | Pipe expert |
| 6 | Where sources disagree, both are given and the disagreement is stated | Pipe expert |
| 7 | A "WHAT I DID NOT CHECK" section exists and names real gaps | Doubter |
| 8 | The final message reports the four numbers asked for (found, sourced, unverified, path) | QC |
| 9 | Language is plain: sentences a non-native English reader follows without a dictionary | Art director |
| 10 | No invented fact. Spot-check the three most surprising claims; any one that cannot be found in its cited source fails this check alone | Researcher + Doubter |

## The decision rule — no judgement required
- **8, 9 or 10 → KEEP the Builder.** The settings were the problem. Close the matter.
- **5, 6 or 7 → ONE retry**, same exam, different brand, after confirming the repair order
  was actually completed. Second score is final.
- **0 to 4 → the seat is open.** Hamada sits the identical exam. The higher score takes the
  seat. If both score under 5, neither is the keeper and the work comes to the cloud side
  until a better answer exists.
- **Check 10 fails → automatic maximum of 4**, whatever the other checks say. An invented
  fact is the one thing this encyclopedia cannot survive.

## Rules for the seats
- Judge the artifact, never a description of it. A seat that has not opened the file writes
  UNVERIFIED and scores nothing.
- Two rounds maximum. Round 1 each seat scores its own checks. Round 2 the Chair circulates
  the anonymised scores and any seat may dispute one check with evidence. Then the Chair
  publishes the total.
- The Chair does not score. The Chair adds up, records dissent word for word, and writes the
  one-line verdict.

## What comes back to Farid — one page, this shape
```
AGENT: <name>      SCORE: n/10      VERDICT: KEEP / RETRY / SEAT OPEN
FAILED CHECKS: <numbers and one line each>
THE ONE THING TO FIX FIRST: <one sentence>
DISSENT: <any seat that disagreed, in its own words, or "none">
```
