## 5. THE ONE ERROR IN THE PAPER THE COUNCIL DID NOT CATCH
C23 reports a gold band hallmarked **"325 (9 carat gold)"**. British hallmarking recognises
six finenesses — 999, 990, 916, 750, 585 and **375 (9ct)**. **There is no 325.** Not an
invented fact (it is attributed to the page, with the Builder's own caution attached), so
check 10 is not capped — but it is a wrong number sitting next to the only exact-year dating
method we have for banded pipes. Correct it in the cabinet and note it against the source.
Sources: [Assay Office London](https://www.assayofficelondon.co.uk/hallmarking/uk-hallmarks) ·
[Complete guide to British hallmarks](https://www.whatisthismark.com/guides/complete-guide-to-british-hallmarks)
