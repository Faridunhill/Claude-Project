**Subject: Permission request — the A.S.P. Pipe Parts Charts, for a collectors' encyclopedia**

Dear Mr Burney,

My name is Farid Hadid. I am a pipe collector and estate-pipe dealer in New Jersey, and
I am building a free, publicly readable encyclopedia for pipe collectors —
identification, dating, restoration and the history of the houses.

I am writing because your **A.S.P. Pipe Parts Charts** are, in my view, still the
clearest explanation of pipe nomenclature and construction anyone has produced. I found
them on Pipedia, where they appear with your permission and your copyright notice
intact, and I did not want to do anything with them without asking you first.

**What I would like to ask permission for:** to reproduce the Pipe Parts Charts within
the encyclopedia's anatomy section, unaltered, with your copyright line, your name and a
link to a destination of your choosing displayed on the same page as the images.

I want to be equally clear about a few things:

- **This is a free, non-subscription resource.** The encyclopedia is not behind a paywall
  and there are no advertisements on it.
- **I will not alter, crop, re-letter or re-draw your charts**, and I will not present
  them as our work.
- **If you would rather say no, that is completely fine** — I will simply cite the charts
  as a reference and commission original diagrams instead. I would rather have your good
  opinion than your artwork.
- If you would prefer a different arrangement — a licence fee, a limited number of
  charts, a specific credit wording, or a link to your own pages instead of a
  reproduction — I would be glad to follow whatever you propose.

One more thing, offered with no strings attached. Our project has a research archive of
catalogues, trade price lists and period advertising which we are indexing and citing
publicly. If anything in it is ever useful to you, you are welcome to it.

Thank you for the charts. They have taught a great many people, including me, and it
seemed only right to ask before making any use of them.

With respect,

**Farid Hadid**
